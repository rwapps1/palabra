Palabra turns vocabulary reinforcement into something you actually want to open. Quiz yourself, race the clock, flip cards, conjugate verbs — while a 6-box spaced-repetition engine quietly tracks what's sticking and resurfaces what isn't. Use it alongside your classes, textbook, or favorite language app to help new words stick.

Different ways to learn

One app, every angle

Different modes suit different moods — and different words. Palabra gives you all of them in the same place.

🔤

Quiz

Type the translation or pick it from choices — Spanish to English and back again.

⏱

Time Attack

60 seconds on the clock. Answer fast, chain correct answers, beat your best score.

🧩

Memory Match

Flip cards, find the pairs, and let visual recall do the heavy lifting.

📖

Conjugate

Practice present-tense verbs across every pronoun — reflexive verbs included.

🏷️

Categories

16 themed word lists — food, animals, family, weather, clothing, and more.

🏆

Achievements

Unlock badges as your streaks, scores, and word counts add up over time.

📊

My Progress

See exactly which words and verb combos are locked in — and which need review.

Install it like an app. Palabra is a Portable Web App — add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app, no store required.
